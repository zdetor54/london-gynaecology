Care During the Menopause

With regards to menopause care, I can help by evaluating symptoms, recommending appropriate treatments like hormone replacement therapy (HRT), discussing lifestyle changes, and monitoring overall health concerns during menopause, such as bone density and cardiovascular risk. All while delivering customized care based on your specific needs and concerns.

There are important evidence-based resources you can direct people to following your consultation, especially if people want time to learn and reflect more, such as:

  • Menopause Matters
  • Weight Watchers UK
  • Women's Health Concern
  • Daisy Network
  • NHS: Menopause
  • Rock My Menopause
