In recent years, there has been a growing recognition of the importance of optimizing gynecological services to enhance patient care and reduce unnecessary visits. A notable advancement in this area is the establishment of the One-Stop Clinic. I offer my patients a unique approach by integrating transvaginal ultrasound into routine examinations, providing out-patient hsyteroscopy and colposcopy all at once if necessary, with immediate follow-ups. This enables me to assess various gynecological conditions more accurately and swiftly, resulting in a more efficient, patient-centered care. This comprehensive approach not only streamlines the diagnostic process but also minimizes the need for multiple follow-up appointments, leading to a more efficient and stress-free healthcare experience. As a consultant gynecologist specializing in diagnostic gynecologic oncology procedures, I provide comprehensive care for the initial diagnosis of gynecological cancers, including ovarian, cervical, and uterine cancers. I am a fellow of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists and hold a master's degree in medical ultrasound from King's College London.

Additionally, I have completed advanced training in the principles of menopause care at University College London. This training has been immensely beneficial for my patients experiencing post-menopausal bleeding while on hormone replacement therapy (HRT), as they can be cared for by someone knowledgeable in both advanced menopause care and the diagnostic approaches to gynecological cancers.

My practice is grounded in providing expert advice and up-to-date care while ensuring my services align with the holistic needs of my patients.
